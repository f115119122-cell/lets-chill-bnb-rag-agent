## Booking deposit and normal cancellation 訂金與一般取消
A booking is confirmed only after the guest pays a 30% booking deposit by bank transfer within 3 days.
The rest is paid at check-in by cash, credit card or LINE Pay.
Cancelling 14 or more days before check-in: the deposit is refunded in full.
Cancelling 7 to 13 days before check-in: 50% of the deposit is refunded.
Cancelling 6 days or less before check-in, or not arriving: the deposit is not refunded.
One date change is free if it is made 14 or more days before check-in; the new date must be within 90 days.
Refunds are sent by bank transfer within 7 working days.
訂房須於 3 天內匯款 30% 訂金才算完成，尾款於入住時付清（現金、信用卡、LINE Pay）。
入住日前 14 天以上取消：訂金全額退還；前 7～13 天：退 50%；前 6 天內取消或未到：不退。
入住日前 14 天以上可免費改期一次，新日期須在 90 天內。退款於 7 個工作天內匯回。

## Typhoon before arrival: changing or cancelling 颱風：入住前改期或取消
Common questions: A typhoon is coming before my trip or on my check-in day. Can I get my booking deposit back, or move my stay?
常見問法：颱風要來了／入住當天有颱風，訂金可以退嗎？可以改期嗎？
Typhoon rules follow the official warnings of the Central Weather Administration (CWA, 中央氣象署), not forecasts or news.
If the CWA issues a land typhoon warning (陸上颱風警報) that covers Pingtung County on the check-in day or the day before,
the guest may change the date for free (within 90 days) or cancel and get the full deposit back.
If the government closes the road to Kenting (Provincial Highway 26) on the check-in day, the same rule applies.
A sea typhoon warning only (海上颱風警報), rain, or a forecast is not enough: the normal cancellation rules apply,
but the guest may change the date once for free.
The guest must tell the host on LINE before 12:00 on the check-in day.
以中央氣象署正式發布的警報為準，不以預報或新聞為準。
入住當天或前一天，氣象署對屏東縣發布陸上颱風警報（或政府封閉台 26 線往墾丁的道路）：可免費改期（90 天內）或全額退還訂金。
只有海上颱風警報、下雨或預報：依一般取消規定，但可免費改期一次。須在入住當天 12:00 前以 LINE 告知房東。

## Typhoon during your stay 颱風：住宿期間
If a land typhoon warning for Pingtung County is issued while you are staying with us:
If you leave early because of the warning, the nights you do not use are refunded in full.
If you cannot leave safely and need to stay longer, each extra night costs 50% of the normal room price.
Outdoor add-ons (snorkeling, stand-up paddle boarding, night tour, beach BBQ) are cancelled and refunded in full.
During a typhoon, please stay inside, close the balcony doors, and do not go to the beach.
If the power goes out, the lobby has emergency lights and drinking water. Power cuts caused by a typhoon are not compensated.
住宿期間屏東縣發布陸上颱風警報：因警報提前退房，未住的夜數全額退費；無法安全離開而續住，每晚以原房價 50% 計算。
戶外加購行程（浮潛、SUP、夜間導覽、海灘烤肉）取消並全額退費。颱風期間請待在室內、關好陽台門、不要去海邊。
停電時大廳有緊急照明與飲用水；颱風造成的停電不另外補償。

## Security deposit at check-in 入住押金
At check-in, each room pays a refundable security deposit (押金) of NT$1,000, in cash or as a credit card hold.
This is not the same as the booking deposit (訂金), which is part of the room price.
At check-out, the staff check the room within 30 minutes. If nothing is damaged or missing, cash is returned at once,
and a credit card hold is released within 7 working days.
Money is taken from the security deposit only for the items in the damage price list, the cleaning fees, or a lost key card.
If the cost is more than NT$1,000, the guest pays the difference at check-out.
入住時每間房收取可退還的押金 NT$1,000（現金或信用卡預授權），與訂房的訂金不同（訂金是房價的一部分）。
退房時工作人員於 30 分鐘內檢查房間，無損壞或遺失即退還現金；信用卡預授權於 7 個工作天內取消。
押金只會扣除賠償價目表、清潔費或遺失房卡的費用；超過 NT$1,000 時，於退房時補差額。

## Damage and compensation price list 損壞賠償價目表
Normal wear is never charged. Accidental and deliberate damage are charged by this list:
Lost room key card: NT$200. Broken glass or mug: NT$150. Lost or broken TV or air conditioner remote: NT$500.
Towel with a stain that cannot be washed out: NT$300. Bed sheet or duvet cover with such a stain: NT$800.
Broken furniture, lamps, appliances, windows or doors: the real repair or replacement cost, with the receipt shown to the guest.
The host sends photos of any damage to the guest within 24 hours. A guest who disagrees can reply on LINE,
and the host Amy decides the final amount.
正常使用的耗損不收費。意外或故意損壞依下列價目賠償：
遺失房卡 NT$200；打破玻璃杯或馬克杯 NT$150；遺失或損壞電視、冷氣遙控器 NT$500；
毛巾有洗不掉的污漬 NT$300；床單或被套有洗不掉的污漬 NT$800；
家具、燈具、電器、門窗損壞：依實際維修或更換費用，並出示收據。
房東會在 24 小時內傳損壞照片給房客；有異議可在 LINE 回覆，最終金額由房東 Amy 決定。

## Smoking and special cleaning fees 違規吸菸與特殊清潔費
Smoking (including e-cigarettes) inside the building: NT$3,000 deep cleaning fee.
A pet (other than a guide dog) found in the room: NT$2,000 cleaning fee.
Large amounts of sand left in the room or the bathtub after the beach: NT$500 cleaning fee.
Fireworks are not allowed anywhere on the property.
These fees are taken from the security deposit.
室內吸菸（含電子菸）：深度清潔費 NT$3,000。房內發現寵物（導盲犬除外）：清潔費 NT$2,000。
從海邊回來在房間或浴缸留下大量沙子：清潔費 NT$500。民宿範圍內禁止燃放煙火。以上費用從押金扣除。

## Overbooking or a room problem: same or better room 超額預訂或房況問題：同級或升等
Sometimes we cannot give the booked room, because of a booking system error, a double booking,
or a problem in the room (for example a broken air conditioner or a water leak).
First, the host offers a room of a higher type at no extra cost (a free upgrade), if one is free.
If only a lower room type is free, the guest may take it and the price difference is refunded,
and breakfast for everyone in the room is free for those nights. The guest may also choose the other-hotel option below.
因訂房系統錯誤、重複訂房或房況問題（例如冷氣故障、漏水）無法提供原訂房型時：
房東先安排更高等級的房型，不加價（免費升等）。
若只剩較低等級房型，房客可選擇入住並退還房價差額，該房所有人當晚早餐免費；房客也可選擇下方的他館安排。

## Overbooking: moving to another hotel 超額預訂：安排至其他旅館
If no room of the same or a higher type is free, the host books a room of the same or a higher level
at a partner hotel within 5 km, and the B&B pays that room in full for the affected nights.
The B&B also pays the taxi to the other hotel and back.
The guest's payment for those nights is refunded in full, and the guest gets a NT$1,000 voucher for a future stay.
Instead, the guest may cancel the whole booking and get every payment back in full.
Only the host can make these arrangements. The online agent cannot book another hotel or give a voucher.
沒有同級以上空房時：房東安排 5 公里內合作旅館的同級以上房間，房費與往返計程車費由民宿支付，
房客這幾晚已付的錢全額退還，另送 NT$1,000 住宿抵用券；或可取消整筆訂單全額退費。只有房東能安排，線上客服不能代訂旅館。
